School Attendance Support Tracker (SAST) is a simple logical system designed to address school absenteeism in Sierra Leone. Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 (Quality Education), the project demonstrates how computational thinking, algorithms, pseudocode, and flowcharts can be used to monitor students' attendance, calculate attendance percentages, and identify learners who may need additional support. It promotes early intervention and improved access to quality education through a simple, accessible, and open-source approach.
# PROJECT BACKGROUND
School absenteeism remains a challenge in many Sierra Leonean communities, affecting students' academic progress. This project proposes a simple system to monitor attendance, calculate attendance rates, and identify students who may need support, contributing to SDG 4 – Quality Education.
# HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
1. Enter Student Details: The teacher enters the student's name and attendance information.


2. Record Attendance: The system records the number of days attended and the total school days.


3. Calculate Attendance: The system automatically calculates the student's attendance percentage.


4. Check Attendance: The system compares the calculated percentage against the 80% attendance threshold.


5. Identify At-Risk Students: Students with attendance below 80% are flagged for supportive intervention.


6. Display Results: The system displays the attendance percentage and the appropriate status, helping teachers identify students who may need assistance.
 # SDG ALIGNMENT 
 The School Attendance Support Tracker (SAST) supports SDG 4, Quality Education, and is most closely linked to Target 4.1, which calls for all girls and boys to complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education. By flagging learners with low attendance early, it helps school staff follow up before those learners fall behind or drop out.
